More signals.
A clearer picture.

Mental health is deeply individual. Our measurements should reflect that.

I am a Ph.D. student in the Halıcıoğlu Data Science Institute at the University of California San Diego, using data science to improve how we measure and address mental health.

My projects span data modalities and algorithms, with a focus on longitudinal analysis, personalization, and translation. There is no universal biomarker for our mental disorder diagnostic categories as they currently stand. Understanding an individual in the context of many biomarkers can help create a more meaningful axis of measurement.

Advised by Drs. Tauhidur Rahman and Benjamin Smarr
