More signals.
A clearer picture.

Mental health is deeply individual and can manifest in unique physiological signatures.

My projects span data modalities and algorithms, with a focus on longitudinal analysis, personalization, and translation. Psychiatric questionnaires have their place, but they miss vital information and ignore the vast heterogeneity within categories. There is no universal biomarker for any of the mental disorder diagnostic categories as they currently stand. Understanding an individual in the context of many biomarkers can help create a more meaningful axis of measurement and inform personalized prevention and treatment.

I can’t think of a more complicated or important problem to move the needle on. Therefore, I am attempting to accumulate the breadth and depth of computational, biological, and behavioral expertise to do just that! If you are similarly passionate about improving how we understand and manage behavioral health, please reach out; challenges like these need strong communities.
